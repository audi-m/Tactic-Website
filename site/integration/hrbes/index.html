Tactic and Zoom

Streamline your communications by linking Tactic with Zoom. Schedule meetings, invite stakeholders, and launch video calls from project dashboards, with attendance, duration, and recordings flowing back into Tactic.

How Tactic works with Zoom

  1. Connect your Zoom account to Tactic via the integration settings.
  2. Schedule project meetings directly from task or milestone views.
  3. Automatically generate Zoom links and send invites to team members.
  4. Record meeting attendance, duration, and summary in Tactic.
  5. Access past meeting logs and recordings from within the project timeline.
